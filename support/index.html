Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea for the ants? Open a support request (a free GitHub account is needed), and you'll get a reply as soon as possible.

Common questions

How do I change what shows above the clock?
Tap the farm once, then tap the gear button.

Why isn't the weather showing?
The app needs your approximate location to fetch the weather. Check Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Tunnel Time, and make sure you're online.

Does it work on other iPhones?
Yes. It's designed for iPhone Duo standing in tent mode on a desk, and it runs on any iPhone in landscape.

Will it drain my battery?
The app slows its animation when nothing much is happening, at night, and in Low Power Mode. For all-day use as a desk clock, keep your iPhone charging.
