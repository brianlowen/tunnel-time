Tunnel Time

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Tunnel Time is made by one independent developer. It doesn't collect, store, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your approximate location (not your precise location) to show your local weather, sunrise, sunset, and season. Your location is sent to Apple's WeatherKit service to get the forecast. It isn't sent anywhere else, and it isn't stored by the developer. Apple handles that request under Apple's privacy policy.

You can turn location off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services. The farm keeps working without it; it just won’t show your local weather.

Data on your device

Your choices, like what shows above the clock, are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details.

Children

The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
